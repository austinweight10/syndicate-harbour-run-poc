# Pitch beats — Syndicate × sports/athleisure Shopify (5–7 min)

**Audience:** London-focused running / athleisure merchant on Shopify  
**Promise:** Live public signals → Insights|Frictions merchandising story — without claiming fake observed demand.

---

### Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:45)
“This weekend London runners cluster around Hyde Park, Battersea and Clapham. We didn’t invent that — Findarace and RunThrough list it. Syndicate treats those as **AGGREGATE_PROXY** demand *drivers*, then joins them to your catalogue affinities — trainers, race-day, hydration, layers.”

### Beat 2 — Persona (0:45–1:30)
Sketch the **London taper / social runner**: Hackney ↔ Marylebone ↔ Kensington, mid-premium budget, parks + coffee culture. They’ll hit Running Late or Tracksmith Morning Miles mid-week, race Saturday, brunch with London Social Runners. Geo boroughs become join keys, not vanity demographics.

### Beat 3 — Events → Insights (1:30–2:45)
Show 3 live events:
1. **Hyde Park 5K/10K** (Sat 26 Sep) → Insight: race-day + trainers spike  
2. **Victoria Park** (3 Oct, Hackney) → East London affinity cluster  
3. **Royal Parks Half** (11 Oct) → longer taper window → recovery + layers  

Narrate edges: `Event --AFFINITY--> SKU types`. Call out: **not OBSERVED** until Shopify orders land.

### Beat 4 — Cafés as PROXY venues (2:45–3:45)
Flip to run cafés: Running Late (Hackney), Tracksmith (Marylebone), Founders RC (Regent’s Park).  
Edges: `CafeMeetup --VENUE_IN--> Geo`.  
Friction angle: “Your PDP doesn’t know Hackney café culture exists — Syndicate can surface local collections / shipping copy / UGC without stalking social DMs.”

### Beat 5 — Weather friction (3:45–4:30)
Open-Meteo London 7-day: dry race Saturday, then **Sun–Mon precip ~3.7–4.4 mm / ~27–37%**.  
Insight: push **shell / layers** creative *before* Monday wet.  
Friction: static autumn merchandising misses a 48-hour weather window.

### Beat 6 — Insights | Frictions board (4:30–5:45)
- **Insight:** Weekend race cluster + Royal Parks taper = race-day + recovery assortment  
- **Insight:** Café-run geos (Hackney, Marylebone, Kensington) = local collection / store-locator story  
- **Friction:** Catalogue has shells but no weather- or race-tied trigger  
- **Friction:** Treating Meetup/café signals as purchases would overclaim — Syndicate labels **PROXY vs OBSERVED** so merchandisers trust the graph  

### Beat 7 — Close / ask (5:45–6:30)
“Connect your Shopify SKUs once; we keep public London running signals fresh. Next demo: join these PROXY edges to a real order stream and watch Insights promote, Frictions flag gaps — still British English, still provenance-honest.”
